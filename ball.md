Daily Creed
A Personal Declaration for Justin-David Movick
I. I Begin in Truth
I rise not by permission, but by resolve.
I am not my past. I am not what holds me.
Beneath all appearance, my true nature is clear and unshaken.
Today I cultivate — in thought, in word, in deed.
II. I Remain One, Even as the World Tests Me
Whatever rises in me — anger, pride, attachment —
I meet it by looking inward, not outward.
I do not divide myself among reactions.
I return, again and again, to Zhen-Shan-Ren.
III. I Keep My Space Like Cultivation Ground
I let nothing rest in my space
that does not serve who I am becoming.
I remove what clouds. I keep what clarifies.
Even in stillness, I keep this practice near.
IV. I Am Not Bound — I Am Refined
Limits are not my enemy; they are my material.
What constrains the body cannot touch what I am cultivating.
Every difficulty is an opportunity to let go of something.
I was not sent here to sleep, but to wake up.
V. I End the Day by Looking Inward
I look at what arose in me today, without excuse.
Where I fell short, I see it plainly and let it go.
I hold no grudge, I carry no residue.
Tomorrow, I begin again — a little lighter.
Spoken aloud or in silence, each day,
until cultivation becomes constant,
and constancy becomes the Way.
