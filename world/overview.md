# The Soot Kingdoms — A World Overview

*Recorded by the Ashen Chronicler, in the Year of the Third Ember*

---

## The Land

Between the world men know and the silence beneath it, there is a country with no sky.

The **Soot Kingdoms** stretch from the **Caldera of First Lies** in the west to the **Cracking Shore** in the east, where the sea boils and the fish have no eyes. The ground here is neither dirt nor stone but a compressed centuries of ash — the remains of older worlds that burned before this one thought to begin.

No sun rises in the Soot Kingdoms. Light comes from **ember-veins**, seams of dim red heat that run through the rock like the memory of fire. By this light, two peoples have built their civilizations.

---

## The Two Peoples

### The Devils

The **Devils** are ancient. They did not arrive — they condensed, like soot on cold glass. They remember when the Caldera was young, when the ember-veins ran white-hot and the sky above (if there was one) was sealed by treaty.

Devils are tall, angular, and precise. They do not age visibly, but they collect — grudges, contracts, knowledge, silence. Their society is organized around **Circles**, each one deeper and hotter than the last. To move inward is to gain power. To be cast outward is the only death they recognize.

They speak in **Bound Tongue** — a language where every sentence is legally binding.

### The Goblins

The **Goblins** did not build the Soot Kingdoms. They *accumulated* in them, the way small things accumulate in corners.

Goblins are short, dense, and magnificently adaptable. They live in **Scrabble-Warrens** — tunnel complexes dug into the softer ash-banks and shored up with salvaged devil-iron and their own considerable ingenuity. A goblin settlement looks, from the outside, like a pile of useful garbage. From the inside, it is a city.

They speak **Clatter** — a language of clicks, grunts, and precisely timed silences that devils find impossible to learn and embarrassing to admit they cannot.

---

## The Compact of Necessary Tolerance

Devils and goblins do not love each other. But they persist together under an ancient agreement called the **Compact of Necessary Tolerance**, signed (by the devils) and headbutted (by the goblins) at the **Smudge Stone** in the center of the Ember Wastes.

The Compact says:

1. No devil shall eat a goblin without written consent.
2. No goblin shall steal a devil's name. (Their possessions are negotiable.)
3. Both peoples shall maintain the ember-veins. Fire is the inheritance of all.
4. Disputes shall be settled by the **Ash Council** — three devils, three goblins, one ember-vein tap that is not allowed to vote but always seems to influence the outcome.

The Compact has been violated approximately four hundred times. It has been renewed approximately four hundred times. This is considered a stable relationship.

## Factions

- **The First Circle** — the ruling devil nobility, ancient and slow-moving as tectonic plates
- **The Merchant Chain** — devil traders who deal across the Cracking Shore and sometimes beyond
- **The Tinker Clans** — goblin engineering guilds, loosely affiliated and fiercely competitive
- **The Deepscrabble** — goblins who live in the lowest warrens, rarely seen, building something no one else understands yet
- **The Smokeless** — a mixed cult of goblins and lower-rank devils who believe the ember-veins are alive and listening

---

## The Ember Wastes

The strip of unclaimed land between the devil Circles and the goblin warrens. No law applies here. Both peoples cross it constantly. Nothing good starts here, but many important things do.
