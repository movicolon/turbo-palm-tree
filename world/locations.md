# Locations of the Soot Kingdoms

*A geographic record, assembled from devil Registers and goblin oral tradition*

---

## The First Circle

The innermost of the devil Circles, closest to the **Caldera of First Lies** at the Soot Kingdoms' western extreme. The First Circle is not a circle in the geometric sense but a status demarcation — the territory and architecture controlled by the highest-ranking devils.

Its buildings are cut directly into the caldera walls: smooth-faced chambers of basalt, lit by controlled ember-vein channels, connected by high-ceilinged corridors that are deliberately too tall for goblins to reach the ceiling. This is considered an aesthetic choice and a social statement simultaneously.

**Notable within the First Circle:**
- Malachar's Audience Hall — no furniture except one very large chair, deliberately positioned so that everyone else stands
- The Register Room — Dusk-Elowen's archive, cold, quiet, unmarked by any exterior sign
- The Compact Alcove — where the original Compact document is kept under glass, the devil signatures readable, the goblin headbutt-mark still visible as an indentation in the parchment's edge

---

## The Outer Circles

The Second and Third Circles, arranged concentrically outward from the First. Less prestigious, less hot, more populated.

The **Outer Circle Detention Facilities** — Tormenex's domain — are cut into the boundary wall between the Third Circle and the Ember Wastes: far enough out to be peripheral, close enough to the First Circle's authority to be enforceable.

---

## The Third Warren

The largest and most politically significant of the goblin Scrabble-Warrens, located in the ash-banks on the eastern side of the Ember Wastes.

From the outside: an irregular mound of ash-grey material, vented by seventeen exhaust pipes in no visible pattern (the pattern is there; it's a maintenance diagram). Two entrances visible. Seven more not visible.

From the inside: a dense, functional city. The main tunnel is wide enough for two goblins side by side and runs the full diameter of the warren. Off it branch the residential tunnels, the workshop quarter (Tizzle's domain), the kitchen (Retch's domain), the scout staging area, and the elder's chamber.

The ember-vein taps run hot and steady here — Tizzle's thirty-percent efficiency improvement is felt throughout the warren as consistent warmth even in the outer tunnels.

**Notable within the Third Warren:**
- The Fire-Pot Kitchen — center of the warren's social life
- Tizzle's Workshop — cluttered, specific, do not touch anything
- The Elder's Chamber — Mosswick's room, sparse, hundreds of small knot-cords on the walls
- The Scout Staging Area — maps, Pip-Scrabble's route records, a board of current information in Clatter shorthand

---

## The Ember Wastes

The unclaimed strip between the devil Circles and the goblin warrens. Roughly three hours' crossing at a brisk pace, longer if you are careful.

The ground here is unstable ash-crust over hollow chambers — some of them ancient, some of them newly formed. The ember-veins run through but without the control infrastructure of either side. In places the heat bleeds up through the crust as visible shimmer.

It is not uninhabitable. Ashgravel inhabits it. But it requires attention, and it does not reward carelessness.

**Notable in the Ember Wastes:**
- The Smudge Stone — the marker where the Compact is signed and renewed, a dark basalt pillar about waist-height on a goblin, with a faint smudge on the eastern face that may or may not be the original ratification mark
- The Convergence Chamber — below the central Wastes, accessible via a specific route only Ashgravel knows, where the ember-veins meet in a configuration that does not match any geological model Lucaveth has developed
- The Hidden Taps — a dozen unlabeled ember-vein access points scattered across the Wastes, known to Ashgravel and Pip-Scrabble, used by travelers as warming stops

---

## The Cracking Shore

The eastern boundary of the known Soot Kingdoms, where the ember-heat meets something cold.

The sea here is not water but a dark, dense liquid that steams at the surface and boils where the ember-veins meet it. The shore is a constant low thunder of cracking — the sound of the coast reforming itself as heat and cold negotiate.

The fish have no eyes.

No goblin warren is built within an hour's walk of the Cracking Shore. The Merchant Chain has a depot there, staffed by Vorryn's people, that processes imports from beyond. What comes from beyond varies. The depot logs are among the more surprising documents in Dusk-Elowen's Registers.

Ashgravel has crossed the Shore. He says it is not the edge of the world. He says it is a membrane. He has not said what is on the other side, except that it is relevant.

---

## The Deep Flame Records

Not a place you can walk to but a depth you can descend to.

Beneath Lucaveth's Second Circle chambers, a series of laddered shafts descends into the oldest rock of the Soot Kingdoms, where the geological record is preserved in ash-strata and crystalline formations. The temperature increases with depth. The light decreases.

At the lowest accessible level, there are formations that do not match anything above them — structures in the rock that Lucaveth has documented and that he believes are biological in origin. The Deepscrabble goblins, who go deeper than he does, have not confirmed this to him directly but have not denied it either.

---

## The Deepscrabble Warren

Unknown location. Believed to be below the lowest known goblin warren, accessed via tunnels that branch off the Third Warren's sub-basement and that Sniv officially considers closed.

The Deepscrabble goblins surface occasionally. They trade specific materials that cannot be found elsewhere. They do not stay. They are building something. No one outside their community knows what.

Tizzle suspects she knows, based on the materials they trade. She has not told anyone yet. She is not sure she is right. She is getting surer.
