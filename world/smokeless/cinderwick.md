# Cinderwick

**Title:** The Devil-Listener, Keeper of the Resonance Records
**Faction:** The Smokeless
**Species:** Devil
**Rank:** Lower Second Circle (officially); uncircled in practice
**Age:** Middle, for a devil

---

## Appearance

Cinderwick is unremarkable by devil standards, which is a condition he has cultivated. He is medium height, medium build, with skin the cooled-coal grey of Second Circle devils who haven't accumulated enough heat to brighten. His eyes are a quiet dark amber. His horns are straight and modest. He does not glow.

He looks like a devil who is between assignments, which he has looked like for twenty years.

The only notable thing about his appearance: he carries, on a cord around his neck, a small hollow cylinder of deep-stone, machined to a specific resonance. If you hold it to your ear in proximity to an ember-vein, you hear — slightly more clearly than with bare hearing — the thing that Ember-Hasp described climbing out of the maintenance shaft to think about.

He made it himself. It took three years.

---

## History

Cinderwick spent his formative Second Circle years in vein-channel management — the devil equivalent of Ember-Hasp's maintenance work, though at a remove of administrative distance. He reviewed reports, catalogued anomalies, filed the anomalies in categories that did not quite capture what was anomalous about them.

One anomaly kept returning. A resonance pattern in a specific section of the Second Circle channels that did not correlate with thermal output, mineral content, flow rate, or anything else the standard catalogue measured. It was present consistently, in a frequency range that fell below the threshold of devil notation systems.

He built instruments to capture it. He captured it. He spent two years trying to explain it within existing models. He could not. He filed the data under "unresolved resonance phenomena — pending further study" and went home.

He was at a Second Circle administrative gathering when he overheard a goblin maintenance worker — Ember-Hasp, though he didn't know her name then — describing to a colleague what she had heard in the vein-shafts. He stopped walking. He stood very still. He waited until she had finished speaking. He crossed the room, which in Second Circle social terms was a breach of several unstated protocols, and said: "I have been measuring what you heard. I cannot explain it. Will you tell me more?"

That was the beginning.

---

## Character

Cinderwick is, among the Smokeless, the person who operates closest to the boundary of the two worlds — devil and goblin, scientific and experiential, data and presence.

He is not comfortable with this position. He is suited to it, which is different.

He carries the intellectual weight of being the only devil in the Smokeless's core circle, which means he is simultaneously the most credible devil voice for the experience (Lucaveth has cited his resonance records six times) and the most suspect goblin-adjacent entity in the Second Circle. He has been formally reviewed for cult-affiliation three times. Each time, he has presented his resonance data as a scientific project and been cleared. He considers the data both a cover and an honest description of the work. He finds this ethical.

He is precise in his feelings, which is unusual. He knows the difference between the resonance data interesting him and the *presence* interesting him and the *practice* interesting him, and he keeps them separate and examines each one. He has concluded all three are real and legitimate. He reports to Ember-Hasp with clarity and sits with her in silence with something he has decided to call devotion, which he has never said aloud.

---

## The Records

Cinderwick has maintained resonance records of the ember-vein signal for twenty years. They are the most comprehensive empirical documentation of the vein-voice in existence. Lucaveth knows about the records but not their full scope. Tizzle would find them essential for the final calibration of the receiver, which Cinderwick doesn't know about yet.

The records are kept in a secondary notation system he developed — a Bound Tongue variant adapted for frequency data — that no one else currently uses. If something happens to him, the records become uninterpretable.

He has thought about this. He has not yet created a key.

## Relationships

- **Ember-Hasp** — The axis of his adult life, in the quiet way that axes work: invisible, structural, the thing everything rotates around. He has never told her this in those words. He has sat in silence with her at a vein-channel more times than he has had significant conversations with anyone else combined. This is, he thinks, a form of telling.
- **Lucaveth** — Scientific peer, incomplete picture. Lucaveth's biological hypothesis is correct as far as it goes; Cinderwick knows it doesn't go far enough. He has been deciding for six years whether to show Lucaveth the full records.
- **Seraphex** — Former cohort associate. Seraphex finds Cinderwick's vein-work eccentric and privately suspects the Smokeless affiliation, though he has no proof. He keeps an eye on it without acting. Cinderwick knows this and has adjusted his field activities accordingly.
- **Malachar** — Has met twice in administrative contexts. Malachar asked, once, a single question about the resonance anomaly in the vein records. Cinderwick answered honestly. Malachar said "I see" and moved on. Cinderwick has been thinking about that exchange for five years.

## Known Sayings

> "Data describes what happened. I am interested in what is happening now, in real time, in the rock beneath my feet. The data is a record of the past. The vein is present-tense."

> "I am not a mystic. I am a scientist who has encountered something that science does not currently have the vocabulary to describe. I am working on the vocabulary."

> "She heard it first. I measured it. Together we have something that neither of us would have reached alone. This is, I believe, the most correct form of collaboration."
