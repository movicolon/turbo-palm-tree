# Factions of the Soot Kingdoms

*A guide to the organized powers, loose affiliations, and unacknowledged arrangements that structure life between the Caldera and the Cracking Shore*

---

## The First Circle

**Type:** Devil aristocracy
**Leadership:** Malachar the Fell
**Center of power:** The Caldera, innermost Circle buildings

The governing body of devil society and, by extension, the nominal authority over Compact enforcement in the Soot Kingdoms. The First Circle operates by a seniority system so old and internally consistent that it has never needed to be written down, which means it cannot be formally challenged, which is not a coincidence.

First Circle decisions move slowly, by design. Speed signals uncertainty. The First Circle prefers to be correct eventually over correct immediately.

Their relationship with goblin society is formal, structured, and maintained through Ash Council proceedings. They consider goblins a necessary element of the Soot Kingdoms' functioning and do not fully understand goblin society, which they would not admit and which Sniv is aware of.

**Current concerns:** Vorryn's Merchant Chain growing more autonomous than the First Circle is comfortable acknowledging; Lucaveth's biological ember-vein hypothesis requiring a response they haven't formulated; the Deepscrabble project, about which they have incomplete information.

## The Merchant Chain

**Type:** Devil commercial network
**Leadership:** Vorryn, Lord-Factor
**Center of power:** Distributed — depots at Cracking Shore, at the Wastes boundary, and in three locations beyond the Shore

The economic engine of the Soot Kingdoms and the primary interface between the Soot Kingdoms and whatever exists beyond the Cracking Shore. The Merchant Chain is technically subordinate to the First Circle. In practice, the First Circle depends on the Chain's revenue and trade relationships, which Vorryn knows and has never weaponized, which is itself a form of leverage.

The Chain employs devils, goblins (primarily Tinker Clan members for logistics and engineering), and at least two entities from beyond the Shore whose species is not on record in Dusk-Elowen's Registers, which she has noted and not resolved.

**Current concerns:** Seep's Deepscrabble procurement requests are sourcing materials that don't appear in standard catalogues; the beyond-Shore entities have been asking questions about the ember-veins that Vorryn is recording carefully.

## The Tinker Clans

**Type:** Goblin engineering guilds
**Leadership:** No central leader; Tizzle leads the Ash-Rivet Clan, the largest
**Center of power:** Distributed across the warrens; heaviest concentration in the Third Warren

The goblin engineering community, organized into competing and occasionally collaborating clans defined by specialty, methodology, and the specific grudges that have accumulated between founders' descendants over several generations.

The Tinker Clans build, maintain, and improve everything in the warrens that requires technical knowledge — ember-vein tap systems, tunnel supports, ventilation, cooking equipment, salvage processing, the tools that goblins use to do everything else. They also supply modified components to the Merchant Chain and, through Tizzle specifically, to the Deepscrabble.

They rank internally by demonstrated capability, not seniority, which produces sharp results and significant interpersonal friction.

**Current concerns:** Tizzle's vein-voice theory, if validated, would restructure what goblin engineering is *for*; demand from Deepscrabble procurement is pushing the Ash-Rivet Clan into component design territory it didn't previously occupy.

## The Deepscrabble

**Type:** Goblin sub-society; separate but not independent
**Leadership:** Vug, Under-Chief
**Center of power:** Unknown exact location; below the Third Warren's sub-basement

The oldest continuous goblin community in the Soot Kingdoms, though "oldest" is contested because the Deepscrabble's own historical records go deeper than anyone can verify from the surface.

They interact with the surface primarily through trade — Seep's procurement runs, irregular exchanges of deep-materials for surface components. They participate in no political structure above the level of Sniv's Third Warren, to which they have a loose affiliation by geography and a complicated relationship by history.

Most surface goblins know they exist. Most surface goblins don't think about them often. This is, from the Deepscrabble's perspective, approximately correct.

**Current concerns:** The project is entering its final phase. When the receiver is complete and activated, the signal it receives will be audible to anyone in the Soot Kingdoms near an ember-vein. Vug has assessed the consequences. She has decided to proceed. She is prepared to be responsible for what follows.

## The Smokeless

**Type:** Mixed-species contemplative order; technically a cult, in the sense that it has a coherent theology, though the members find the word reductive
**Leadership:** Ember-Hasp (goblin, founder); Cinderwick (devil, co-founder)
**Center of power:** No fixed location; gatherings move

The most unusual faction in the Soot Kingdoms: a voluntary association of goblins and devils organized around a shared practice of listening to ember-veins.

They do not recruit. They do not proselytize. They do not seek political power, economic influence, or institutional recognition. The First Circle has reviewed them three times for seditious potential and found nothing to act on, which they find mildly frustrating.

Members range from a dozen to several dozen depending on season and circumstance, drawn from both sides by the experience of having heard something in the veins that their respective societies had no framework for.

**Current concerns:** Tizzle's vein-voice research and Cinderwick's resonance records are converging with the Deepscrabble project in ways the Smokeless can feel but cannot yet fully articulate. Ember-Hasp has increased the frequency of gatherings. She has not explained why.

## The Ash Council

**Type:** Governing body; dispute resolution; the formal structure of the Compact
**Composition:** Three devil representatives (currently: Seraphex, Dusk-Elowen, and a rotating Second Circle appointee); three goblin representatives (currently: Sniv, and two senior warren delegates); one non-voting ember-vein tap
**Meeting place:** The Smudge Stone and the neutral chamber built around it

The official joint governing body of the Soot Kingdoms. Meets quarterly and in emergency sessions.

The Council's decisions are binding on both populations under Compact law, which means they require unanimous ratification — a structural feature that makes every decision a negotiation and prevents any single party from governing unilaterally.

Seraphex chairs the meetings. Sniv has never formally objected to this. She manages it through preparation — she knows what Seraphex will propose before he proposes it, and her positions are always ready.

The ember-vein tap does not vote, technically. At three Council sessions in living memory, its behavior — pressure fluctuation, heat output change, a sound — has caused the Council to reconsider a decision. All three times the reconsidered decision was different from the original. No one has officially acknowledged the tap's influence. No one has disconnected it.

## The Beyond-Shore Entities

**Type:** Unknown; classification pending
**Known members:** At least two, unnamed in public records
**Center of power:** Beyond the Cracking Shore

Vorryn's trading partners from outside the known world. They deal in materials and information that don't exist in the Soot Kingdoms' catalogues. They have been asking about the ember-veins with increasing specificity.

Dusk-Elowen has a file. It is not complete. She is aware it is not complete. This is among the three things in the Soot Kingdoms that she cannot fully account for, and it occupies more of her attention than she shows.

What they want is not yet known. What they know is not yet known. That they are paying attention is certain.
