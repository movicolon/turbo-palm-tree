# Seraphex

**Title:** Duke of Deceptions, Voice of the First Circle
**Rank:** First Circle, Second Tier
**Age:** Considerable

---

## Appearance

Seraphex is built for rooms. He fills them — not with size, but with presence, the way a low note on a pipe organ fills a hall before it can be identified as music.

His face is handsome by devil standards: sharp angles, deep-set eyes the color of burning copper, a mouth that always appears to be on the edge of saying something that will change your afternoon. His horns curve inward rather than out, which is unusual among devils and which he has never explained.

He dresses in layered blacks and deep reds, always immaculate, always slightly too formal for the occasion. This is intentional.

---

## History

Seraphex rose through the Second Circle by doing what Second Circle devils do: collecting obligations. He is extraordinarily good at it. He has a memory for what people want and an uncanny sense for when they are most willing to pay for it.

He became Malachar's voice not through appointment but through a gradual process of being so useful that removing him became unthinkable. This is, Seraphex believes, the only reliable form of job security.

He has never attempted to surpass Malachar. Not because he couldn't — he suspects he couldn't, but he has never tested it — but because the attempt would end the arrangement, and the arrangement is the most interesting thing in the Soot Kingdoms.

---

## Character

Seraphex is, in his own assessment, honest. He is not lying about things when he speaks. He is simply presenting information in the most useful sequence, with the most appropriate emphasis, for the most productive outcome.

Others call this deception. Seraphex finds the accusation philosophically imprecise.

He genuinely enjoys conversation. He finds goblins, who communicate in Clatter, fascinating rather than frustrating — there is something in the compression of goblin language that appeals to his aesthetic.

He drinks **ember-distillate** — a hot, smoky liquor made from ash-filtered minerals — and does so openly, which among First Circle devils signals a certain studied approachability.

---

## Function

Seraphex negotiates. He writes contracts that appear generous and contain a single clause, buried in the middle of page four, that is not. He mediates disputes between devils and goblins at the Ash Council, performing impartiality so perfectly that neither side has yet realized he is steering outcomes.

He is also Malachar's memory for social obligations — who has been offended, who is owed a favor, who is building toward something and doesn't know it yet.

---

## Relationships

- **Malachar** — Serves without subservience. This is an art form.
- **Lucaveth** — Old rivalry. They were in the same cohort during the formative age. Seraphex was smoother; Lucaveth was deeper. They have never resolved which matters more.
- **Pip-Scrabble** — The goblin scout is one of Seraphex's best informants, though Pip doesn't know that Seraphex knows that Pip is reporting to three different parties simultaneously. Seraphex finds this admirable.

## Known Sayings

> "The truth is the most convincing story. Use it carefully."

> "Every silence is an offer. The question is who accepts it first."

> "I have never lied to you. Think carefully about what that means."
