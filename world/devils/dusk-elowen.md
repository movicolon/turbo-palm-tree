# Dusk-Elowen

**Title:** Lady of Spite, Keeper of the Outer Registers
**Rank:** First Circle, Third Tier (positioned there by choice; technically eligible for higher)
**Age:** Ancient. She stopped counting. The counting was her idea.

---

## Appearance

Dusk-Elowen is beautiful in the way that a cold thing is beautiful — the beauty that makes you careful.

Her skin is the deep blue-black of the sky just after a fire has gone out, shot through with faint silver lines that are not veins but look like them. Her eyes are completely silver. Her hair, which she wears long and unbound, moves as though there is always a wind where she is standing.

She dresses in the colors of the Outer Registers: grey, charcoal, the particular black of old ink. She wears no decoration except a single iron ring on her right hand, from which the word *REMEMBER* has been worn smooth by decades of her thumb.

---

## History

Dusk-Elowen was the first Keeper of the Outer Registers, a position she created for herself during the early age when the First Circle was in administrative chaos and no one was bothering to write things down.

She wrote them down.

The Outer Registers are not a document. They are a *library* — sixty-four iron-bound volumes containing every contract, every violation, every amendment, every broken promise and fulfilled one made in the Soot Kingdoms since the Compact was signed. Dusk-Elowen has read all of them. She has read some of them many times.

She remembers everything. This is a common trait among devils. What makes her singular is that she *references* everything — at the precise moment when it will do the most damage.

---

## The Spite

The title is hers and she chose it.

Spite, in Dusk-Elowen's taxonomy, is not petty. It is the long application of memory to injustice. It is the patient work of ensuring that what was promised is delivered, that what was broken is repaired, that what was taken is returned — not quickly, not loudly, but *eventually*, and with interest.

She has been waiting on three outstanding debts for more than a century. She is not impatient. She is precise.

---

## Character

Dusk-Elowen is the First Circle's institutional memory, and she knows it, which means she also knows that she is the most powerful person in the room who is never considered the most powerful person in the room.

She finds this useful.

She speaks rarely and exactly. She has a gift for the correct word, where *correct* means *the word that is impossible to misunderstand and impossible to deny*.

She has no close allies. She has *records on* everyone.

---

## Relationships

- **Malachar** — They have worked together since before the Compact. She knows things about him that are not in any Register. He knows that she knows. They have never discussed it. This is, in its way, a form of intimacy.
- **Seraphex** — She finds him competent and untrustworthy in ways she has not yet fully catalogued. He finds her terrifying and would never say so. Both assessments are correct.
- **Tormenex** — Provides him with historical precedents for his punishments. This is the closest she comes to having a working friendship with anyone.
- **Scuttle** — The goblin messenger is the only person who regularly enters her Register room. She has never asked why. She knows why.

---

## The Register Room

A chamber cut into the rock just inside the First Circle's innermost boundary. No ember-veins run through it — the heat would damage the paper. It is cold and very quiet. The sixty-four volumes are arranged in a way that looks random and is not.

No one is permitted inside without Dusk-Elowen's presence. No copy exists of any Register. The copies exist only in her.

## Known Sayings

> "Spite is just memory with motivation."

> "Everything you've done is here. Everything you've not yet done is also, in outline, here."
