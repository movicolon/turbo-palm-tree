# Ashgravel

**Title:** The Wandering, The Unassigned
**Rank:** Technically Second Circle; functionally uncircled
**Age:** Unknown, even to himself

---

## Appearance

Ashgravel looks like a devil who has been outside for a very long time.

His skin, where it shows, is the color of ash that has been rained on and dried again — a pale, mottled grey-brown without the ember-glow that most devils carry. He has been in the Ember Wastes long enough that the heat has equalized. He no longer radiates.

He is tall but walks bent, as though carrying something on his back that isn't there. His eyes are the dull red-orange of a fire that has been burning long enough to forget it started. His horns are wrapped in strips of goblin-cloth, for reasons he has explained differently to everyone who has asked.

He carries everything he owns on his person. This is less than you'd expect.

---

## History

Ashgravel's history is a collection of other people's stories about him, assembled into a loose shape that he neither confirms nor denies.

He was, it is generally agreed, once a full Second Circle devil with a position, obligations, and a future of the sort that Second Circle devils have. Something ended that. The stories diverge at what the something was — a contract broken, a debt owed, a thing witnessed in the Outer Circles, a journey started for reasons that were clear at the time and became unclear later.

What is agreed: he has been walking the Ember Wastes for a very long time. He knows them the way a river knows a riverbed — not from study but from repetition, from the accumulation of passages.

He has been to the Cracking Shore. He has returned. He doesn't talk about what's beyond it except to say that the fish having no eyes is the most important thing he saw, and that anyone who wants to understand this should think about what there is to see in the dark, and what stops trying.

---

## Character

Ashgravel is quiet in the way that things become quiet after a long noise — not empty, but stilled.

He is helpful to travelers in the Ember Wastes. He knows where the hidden ember-taps are, where the ash-banks are safe to cross, where the crust is thin above old hollow chambers. He offers this without payment, which confuses both devils (who consider it naive) and goblins (who consider it suspicious, then, after a time, moving).

He is not broken. This is important. He has been through things that might have broken him, and the result was not breakage but a kind of reordering — the same material arranged into a different shape.

He does not return to the Circles. He is not forbidden; he simply doesn't go. He says there is enough to do out here.

---

## The Wastes Knowledge

Ashgravel knows three things about the Ember Wastes that no one else knows:

1. There is a chamber beneath the central Wastes where the ember-veins converge. He has been there. He says it is not what Lucaveth thinks it is.
2. The Cracking Shore is not the edge of the world. It is a membrane.
3. The ash remembers. Not metaphorically. He has heard it.

He has shared the first with Lucaveth, who is working through the implications. He has shared the second with no one. He has shared the third with Mosswick, who nodded slowly and said "yes, we know," which was the first time in a very long time that Ashgravel felt less alone.

## Relationships

- **Vorryn** — Shared fires in the Wastes, no contracts. This is trust.
- **Mosswick** — The closest thing to a confidant. They meet irregularly at an unmarked point in the Wastes that both know without having agreed to know it.
- **Fern-Bite** — The young goblin follows Ashgravel sometimes on forays into the outer Wastes. He lets them. He was young once, in whatever way applies.
- **Pip-Scrabble** — Knows Ashgravel has information. Has been trying to extract it for three years. Has not succeeded. This is not for lack of trying.

## Known Sayings
