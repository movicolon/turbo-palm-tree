# Vorryn

**Title:** The Iron-Tongued, Lord-Factor of the Merchant Chain
**Rank:** Second Circle (exiled from First by personal choice)
**Age:** Middle, for a devil — still accumulating

---

## Appearance

Vorryn looks like a devil who has decided being a devil is simply one of many useful features. He is compact by devil standards, built more like a forge than a cliff — dense, functional, radiating heat that you notice only after you've been standing near him for a while.

His skin runs deep red-brown, warmer than most devils'. His horns are worn down at the tips — not from violence but from ducking under low goblin tunnel ceilings, which he does constantly and without complaint.

He carries a ledger. Always. Its cover is iron-bound goblin-leather, and it has never been seen closed.

---

## History

Vorryn was born — or condensed, as devils say — in the First Circle. He found it boring.

The First Circle is about power. Vorryn is about something more interesting: *exchange*. He petitioned to be reassigned to the Merchant Chain at the age when most devils are just beginning to accumulate their first grudges. The First Circle considered this eccentric. He considered their consideration irrelevant and left.

He built the Merchant Chain from a loose arrangement of devil traders into a structured network that now touches every ember-vein tap, every goblin salvage depot, and three entities beyond the Cracking Shore whose names do not translate cleanly into either Bound Tongue or Clatter.

---

## Character

Vorryn likes goblins. This is considered mildly scandalous in both communities.

He does not like them sentimentally — he likes them commercially, which is, he would argue, a deeper form of respect. He understands goblin value hierarchies, which are complicated, nonlinear, and involve three categories that don't exist in devil economics. He has adapted his pricing accordingly.

He is loud in a way other devils are not. He laughs, which is unusual. He argues with vendors, which is considered beneath his rank. He has been seen eating at goblin food-stalls, which is not prohibited by the Compact but which raises eyebrows at the First Circle.

His word, once given in trade, is absolute. His prices, once set, are non-negotiable. These two facts together make him simultaneously the most trustworthy and the most infuriating trading partner in the Soot Kingdoms.

---

## The Iron Tongue

The title refers not to cruelty but to precision. Vorryn speaks in numbers and terms with the same binding weight that Malachar speaks in decrees. A Vorryn price is a Vorryn price. No appeal, no amendment, no re-offer.

The goblin Tinker Clans call him **Klak-voss** — approximately "the one who bites fair" — which is a significant compliment.

---

## Relationships

- **Malachar** — Respectful distance. Malachar needs Vorryn's network. Vorryn needs Malachar's authority behind his contracts. Neither will admit this to the other.
- **Tizzle** — Vorryn's most reliable goblin supplier. He considers Tizzle a peer and has said so, which has made Tizzle simultaneously famous and suspicious in the warrens.
- **Grundmaw** — Has hired the goblin warrior for security on three cross-waste supply runs. Considers Grundmaw expensive and worth it.
- **Ashgravel** — Fellow traveler in the Ember Wastes. They have shared fires without contracts, which among devils is a form of trust that predates language.
