# Malachar the Fell

**Title:** Archduke of the First Circle, Keeper of the Caldera Seal
**Rank:** First Circle, Innermost Tier
**Age:** Unmeasured. Older than the Compact. Possibly older than the goblin concept of "old."

---

## Appearance

Malachar is tall the way a cliff-face is tall — not impressively, but unavoidably. His skin is the color of cooled lava: grey-black with orange cracks at the joints that glow faintly when he is displeased, which is most of the time.

He wears no crown. He has explained, once, that a crown is a request for acknowledgment. He does not request.

His eyes are two different temperatures. The left runs cold — almost white. The right burns amber. Which one you find yourself looking at determines, in ways no one has been able to fully document, what kind of conversation you are about to have.

---

## History

Malachar did not rise to power. He was already there when the concept of power arrived and organized itself around him.

He signed the **Compact of Necessary Tolerance** in the early age. The goblins' headbutt representative at the signing left a smudge on his robes. He has never mentioned it. The robe is still in his wardrobe, unwashed, in a place of honor.

He has been challenged for leadership of the First Circle seven times. Six challengers were defeated and reassigned to the outer edges of the Kingdom. The seventh came extremely close and was given, as a mark of respect, an administrative post in the Merchant Chain, where he has been quietly miserable ever since.

---

## Character

Malachar does not lie. This is not virtue — it is leverage. Every word he says is true, and he arranges truths the way a stonemason arranges stones: so that the shape they form is not the shape you expected.

He is patient in a way that is difficult to witness. He has been known to sit in silence for three days during a negotiation, watching the other party exhaust themselves with offers.

He is not cruel by preference. Cruelty is imprecise and tends to generate administrative problems.

---

## Relationships

- **Seraphex** — His second. Malachar trusts Seraphex the way a master locksmith trusts a fine pick: absolutely, within its function, and never with anything else.
- **Vorryn** — Views the merchant devil with mild contempt and significant financial dependence. Has never acknowledged the second fact.
- **Mosswick** — The only goblin he addresses by name. This is considered a tremendous honor in the warrens and a minor lapse in protocol at the First Circle. Malachar is aware of both reactions and finds them both correct.
