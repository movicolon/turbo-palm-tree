# Pip-Scrabble

**Title:** Chief Scout of the Third Warren, Runner of the Ember Wastes
**Rank:** Scout-Second (reports to Sniv directly; technically should report to the Scout-First, who reports to Sniv, but the Scout-First finds Pip-Scrabble difficult to supervise)
**Age:** Young adult, though it's hard to tell with scouts

---

## Appearance

Pip-Scrabble is fast, which is her defining physical characteristic. She is built for it: lean, compact, long-limbed for a goblin, with feet that seem to find purchase on surfaces other goblins slide off. Her ears are larger than average and independently mobile, which she uses to orient in dark tunnels and which makes her look, to devils, perpetually alarmed.

Her skin is a pale greyish-green, remarkable for how seldom it gets dirty — she moves through spaces without touching more of them than necessary, which is both a scout's discipline and a personal aesthetic.

She carries almost nothing: a small pack, a short blade, a roll of thin cord, and several small packets of what she calls "trade goods" that Sniv has decided not to examine too closely.

---

## History

Pip-Scrabble grew up in the Third Warren's outer tunnels, the section closest to the Ember Wastes, where goblins with adventurous temperaments and poor judgment about risk tend to end up spending their childhoods. She became a scout because she was already doing it without the title.

She was officially recruited by the previous Scout-First when she was caught, for the second time, having crossed the Wastes and returned with information that the official scout corps hadn't had yet. The Scout-First's assessment: "We cannot stop her. We should direct her."

She has crossed the Ember Wastes forty-three times by her count. She has entered devil territory without authorization eleven times, which Sniv knows about and Tormenex knows about and neither has so far acted on, which Pip-Scrabble takes as implicit permission while both parties take it as a decision to delay.

---

## Character

Pip-Scrabble is the kind of curious that moves before it thinks and thinks very fast while moving. She collects information the way some goblins collect salvage — compulsively, with no immediate plan for all of it, on the theory that having it is always better than not.

She is genuinely brave, which among goblins is recognized as distinct from recklessness, though her particular flavor of bravery and recklessness are difficult to tell apart in the moment. In retrospect, they are always distinguishable by whether she came back with something useful.

She maintains, simultaneously, reporting relationships with Sniv (her official superior), Seraphex (who she believes is using her; she is correct; she finds this acceptable because she is also using him), and Vorryn (who she trades information for trade-goods access, which she uses to fund further scouting). She has not told any of them about the others. She considers this professional.

---

## The Wastes

Pip-Scrabble knows the Ember Wastes better than any goblin except, possibly, Ashgravel. She and the wandering devil have encountered each other five times out there. She has tried four times to get information from him. He has been friendly, helpful about specific route questions, and precisely uninformative about anything she actually wants to know.

She finds him interesting, which is more than she says about most devils.

---

## Relationships

- **Sniv** — Loyally hers. When it comes to it, Pip's reports to Sniv are the most complete and the most honest.
- **Seraphex** — A relationship of mutual use that both parties are aware of and neither is ready to end.
- **Ashgravel** — Ongoing, inconclusive investigation. She has learned one thing certain: he is not dangerous to goblins.
- **Grundmaw** — They work together on the dangerous crossings. They do not like each other particularly. They work very well together.

## Known Sayings

> "The devils think they know the Wastes because they named it. They don't know it. They've never had to run across it in the dark."
