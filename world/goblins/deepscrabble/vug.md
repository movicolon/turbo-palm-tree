# Vug

**Title:** The Under-Chief, Voice of the Deepscrabble
**Rank:** First among the Deepscrabble; the ranking system below is different from the one above
**Age:** Long. The deep slows things down.

---

## Appearance

Vug is the color of things that have never seen ember-light: a pale, near-translucent greenish-white, with the faint blue-grey of deep rock beneath. Her eyes have expanded — larger than surface goblins, the pupils widened permanently to gather the very little light that exists at depth. In surface light she squints. She finds it excessive.

She moves with the deliberate economy of deep-place creatures. No wasted motion. Every step placed. When she speaks — which is infrequently — it is in a version of Clatter so compressed and low-register that most surface goblins need three repetitions to follow it.

She wears no decorative materials. Everything on her person is functional. This is not austerity — it is the pragmatic logic of a life spent in places where carrying anything unnecessary is a burden you chose.

---

## History

Vug is the third Under-Chief. The first two are commemorated in the deep-place naming tradition: their names have been added as a suffix to the main tunnel's designation, so the tunnel they built is called, in the compressed Deepscrabble dialect, "the way of the first two and the one before Vug." There is no shorter form. Length is considered respectful.

She became Under-Chief by consensus, which in the Deepscrabble takes the form of everyone continuing to defer to you until it becomes official. She has held the position for twenty years. In the deep, this is approximately equivalent to sixty on the surface, which she knows and which explains the slight patience she extends to surface goblins who seem rushed.

The thing the Deepscrabble are building is almost complete. Vug has been overseeing the final phase for the past three years. She surfaces only to trade for specific materials — Tizzle's refined components, primarily. She does not explain what they are for. Tizzle, she suspects, has worked out most of it.

---

## Character

Vug is not mysterious by preference. She is compressed by adaptation. The deep rewards economy of expression, economy of movement, economy of information shared. She has not shed this simply because she has surfaced.

She is not hostile to surface goblins. She finds them loud and slightly exhausting in a way that reads as affectionate once you know her.

She is entirely loyal to the Deepscrabble community and to the project. Not ideologically — she did not invent the project, she inherited it — but pragmatically. The work is what the work is. She has assessed it carefully and concluded it is worth completing.

She has met Mosswick twice at depth. These are the only conversations she has had with surface goblins that did not feel abbreviated. Mosswick, Vug concluded, operates closer to deep-time than any surface creature she has encountered.

---

## The Project

Vug will not describe it except to say: it is a response to a question the Deepscrabble have been asking for five generations. The question is about the ember-veins. Specifically, about what they carry besides heat.

Tizzle calls it the vein-voice. The Deepscrabble have a different word, in their compressed dialect, that translates approximately as "the thing that has been trying to say something."

The construction is a receiver.

---

## Relationships

- **Tizzle** — The most important surface relationship, commercially and intellectually. Vug believes Tizzle is close to understanding what the project is. She has considered explaining it. She keeps deciding to wait until Tizzle arrives there alone, which she believes is more correct.
- **Mosswick** — The only surface goblin she has spoken to about the deep-time concept. Mosswick said "yes." This was, Vug found, the most useful conversation she has had on the surface.
- **Sniv** — Correct, respectful, appropriately cautious. Vug considers Sniv a good chief. She does not say this. Sniv does not ask.
- **Lucaveth** — He is close, in his biological hypothesis. He is missing one thing. Vug has thought about telling him. She has decided the missing thing is something he needs to find rather than be given.

## Known Sayings

> "The deep is not dark. You simply haven't been there long enough for your eyes to change."

> "Every question the surface asks, we asked it five generations earlier. We are still working on the answers. This is not failure. This is the correct pace."

> "The voice in the vein is not speaking to us. It is speaking through us. We are building ears."
