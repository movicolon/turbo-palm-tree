# Chert

**Title:** Builder-First of the Deepscrabble, Hands of the Project
**Rank:** Second among the Deepscrabble; all building decisions pass through him
**Age:** Mid-life, deep-reckoned

---

## Appearance

Chert's hands are his biography. They are large, deeply calloused, stained at the fingertips and in the knuckles with mineral deposits from the specific stones of the deep — ochres and blue-greys that don't exist on the surface. He has lost the tip of one finger to a rock-cut and the nail of another to a collapse. Both injuries are so old they've fully healed into the landscape of his hands.

He is broad and low-built, with the density of something that has been compressed from the outside in. His skin is pale like Vug's but more mottled — he spends time in the transitional tunnels between deep and surface, where the light varies, and his coloring has adapted into a kind of in-between.

He communicates primarily in gesture when working — a shorthand language of hand-signals developed by Deepscrabble builders across generations, more precise for construction than any spoken language and almost completely impenetrable to outsiders.

---

## History

Chert inherited the Builder-First role from his predecessor the way deep things transfer: through continuous presence and demonstrated understanding. He was there. He watched. He built. At some point he was the one who knew most. The role followed.

He has been working on the project for his entire adult life. Not with fanaticism — with craft. He is a builder. The project is the largest and most interesting build he has encountered. He would work on it regardless of its significance. The significance is, for him, secondary to the quality of the construction.

He designed the receiver's core housing — a chamber cut to tolerances that surface goblins would consider impossible, from deep-stone that does not exist in any catalogue of Soot Kingdom geology. The chamber resonates at a specific frequency. Getting it to resonate at that frequency took eleven years and four failed prototypes.

The current version is correct. He knows because of how his hands feel when he touches it. This is not mysticism. It is the knowledge of a builder who has learned to read material through contact.

---

## Character

Chert is the most communicative of the Deepscrabble who surface regularly, which means he speaks in full sentences and occasionally asks questions. He finds surface conversation slightly fast but manageable.

He has strong aesthetic opinions about construction, which are difficult to explain in surface Clatter because the Deepscrabble have vocabulary for structural beauty that the surface dialects lack. He has tried to explain this to Tizzle, who understood more than he expected, which he found moving.

He is not interested in politics, history, or the wider implications of the project. He is interested in whether the receiver is correctly built. When it is complete, his job is done. What happens after is Vug's domain and the project's nature.

---

## Relationships

- **Vug** — Works under her authority without hierarchy problems. They have divided the world correctly: she holds the purpose, he holds the construction. Neither interferes with the other's domain.
- **Tizzle** — The most productive surface relationship he has had. She sent him a refined resonance component last year that was machined to a tolerance he hadn't thought possible at the surface level. He looked at it for a long time before installing it. He sent back, through the trade channel, a piece of deep-stone worked into a form that has no function except to demonstrate what deep-cut craftsmanship looks like. Tizzle has it on her workbench.
- **Seep** — The Deepscrabble's surface trader is his interface to the supply chain. He specifies; Seep procures. He has tried not to think about how Seep gets some of what he asks for. He has not fully succeeded.

## Known Sayings

> "The deep-stone rings true when it's right. You learn to hear it. It takes years. There are no shortcuts and no substitutes."

> "I am not building something to change the world. I am building something that the world made necessary. These are different."
