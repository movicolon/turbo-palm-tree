# Seep

**Title:** Surface Runner of the Deepscrabble, The One Who Goes Up
**Rank:** Third among the Deepscrabble; first among those who leave
**Age:** Younger than Vug and Chert; exactly the right age for what the role requires

---

## Appearance

Seep is the Deepscrabble goblin most adapted to surface conditions, which means she looks almost like a surface goblin — almost. Her eyes are still too large, still too light-gathering, still slightly unsettling to surface creatures who don't know what they're seeing. She has learned to wear a brimmed hat in brighter areas of the warrens to manage this.

Her coloring is mid-range — not the pale translucence of the deepest Deepscrabble, not the darker tones of the surface warrens. She is in-between, which she has cultivated deliberately. In the deep she is identifiable as a surface-runner by her adaptation; on the surface she can, with effort, pass as an unremarkable traveler from the outer warrens.

She moves quickly but not like Pip-Scrabble's constant-transit speed. Seep's quickness is *episodic* — she can be absolutely still and then be somewhere else, which people find more unsettling than simple speed.

---

## History

Seep was identified as a surface-runner candidate at a young age — she had an easier time in the transition tunnels than most Deepscrabble, tolerated the variable light better, recovered faster from the surface's overwhelming sensory register.

She was trained for the role by the previous surface-runner, a goblin named Gravel-Eye who retired to the deep and whose contact network Seep inherited, improved, and in three cases quietly replaced when the original contacts had become compromised by their own entanglements.

She has been running for the Deepscrabble for eight years. In that time she has sourced every component for the project's final phase. Some of these components were straightforward procurement — Tizzle's workshop, Vorryn's merchant depots. Some were more complex. She has a relationship with parties on both the devil side and the goblin side that no one on either side knows the full extent of.

---

## Character

Seep is the most socially functional of the Deepscrabble on the surface, which she holds as a point of professional pride and approaches with the precision of a specialist.

She is fluent in surface Clatter, functional in Bound Tongue, and has a working knowledge of three trade-pidgin variants used in the Merchant Chain depots. She adapted to surface social rhythms faster than any previous surface-runner; she has also retained her deep-pattern interiority better than her predecessors, which means she can code-switch between the compressed deep mode and the expansive surface mode without visible seam.

She does not tell people what she is. She is not required to and it would complicate procurements. She is not ashamed of it. If directly asked, she answers honestly. She is almost never directly asked.

She is the only person in the Soot Kingdoms who knows both Pip-Scrabble's full intelligence network and the Deepscrabble's complete supply chain. She has kept both entirely separate. This is among the more impressive feats of information management currently operating.

## Relationships

- **Vug** — Reports to. Complete trust, earned and tested. Vug gave her the role and has never second-guessed a procurement decision, which Seep finds the most validating form of confidence.
- **Chert** — He specifies; she procures. The relationship is precise and functional. He once sent her a component specification that she considered impossible. She returned it in thirty days. He has not questioned a specification since.
- **Tizzle** — The most direct surface relationship. Tizzle knows she's Deepscrabble. They have reached a mutual agreement of professional curiosity — Tizzle asks about the components' purpose, Seep listens carefully and says "not yet," which Tizzle accepts because she is used to sitting with incomplete information.
- **Pip-Scrabble** — Parallel operators. They have met three times in the field without either acknowledging what the other was doing. The third time, Pip left a small route-marker for Seep at a dangerous crossing, which required knowing Seep's route, which Seep had told no one. She has not yet decided how to respond to this. She finds it interesting.
- **Scuttle** — Uses his message network for certain communications. He doesn't ask about the messages. This is the basis of their relationship.
