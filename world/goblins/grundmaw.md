# Grundmaw

**Title:** Warmaster of the Third Warren, Shield of the Compact
**Rank:** Warren-Fighter, First Grade
**Age:** Prime fighting age, which for Grundmaw has been lasting a surprisingly long time

---

## Appearance

Grundmaw is the largest goblin in the Third Warren and one of the largest in any warren. He stands almost to the shoulder of a mid-ranking devil, which is unusual enough that it is noted in Dusk-Elowen's Registers under the entry for "goblins of notable physical specification."

His skin is deep, dark green, almost black in low ember-light. He has no tusks — they were knocked out in his first major fight, a point of history he neither hides nor discusses — and instead has a broad, flat jaw that is, structurally speaking, approximately as hard as the iron he salvages.

He carries a two-handed war-bar — not a weapon found in any catalogue, but a length of solid devil-iron, head-thick and arm-long, that he found half-buried in the outer ash-banks and which he has been carrying ever since. No one has ever asked him to put it down.

He has significant scarring across his left shoulder and upper arm, from the border dispute of twelve years ago. He declined the offer to have it treated into invisibility. It is, he says, a map of something true.

---

## History

Grundmaw did not choose to be a warrior. He chose to be effective, and the intersection of his size, his temperament, and the Third Warren's needs made warrior the most effective thing he could be.

He has fought in the border dispute, in three incidents involving entities crossing from beyond the Cracking Shore, and in one internal warren conflict that Sniv has declared officially not to have happened. He has won all of these engagements. He has not always been happy about it.

He was offered the Warren-Chief position twice — once after the border dispute, once after the Cracking Shore incidents. He declined both times on the grounds that he would be worse at it than Sniv and didn't see the point of doing something he'd be worse at.

---

## Character

Grundmaw is quiet. Not strategic-quiet like Sniv, or patient-quiet like Mosswick — just actually quiet. He has found that most things do not require as many words as people give them, and that the things that require no words at all are often more important.

He is loyal to the Third Warren with an uncomplicated depth that is unusual in a world of complex allegiances. He doesn't have multiple reporting relationships. He doesn't trade information. He protects what he has been given to protect.

He is not stupid. People sometimes assume this because he is large and quiet, and he lets them, because an underestimated Grundmaw is a more effective Grundmaw.

He has strong opinions about the quality of iron in the devil foundries versus goblin salvage iron. This is the subject on which he is most likely to become verbose.

---

## Relationships

- **Sniv** — The line of command he respects completely and without reservation.
- **Pip-Scrabble** — Professional partnership. They disagree on methods (Pip approaches danger obliquely; Grundmaw approaches it directly) and work excellently in combination.
- **Vorryn** — Three joint security contracts. Grundmaw considers Vorryn the most tolerable devil he has dealt with directly. Vorryn considers Grundmaw expensive and worth it.
- **Retch** — Grundmaw eats more than anyone else in the warren. Retch accommodates this without comment. In return, Grundmaw has repaired the kitchen's structural supports twice without being asked.

---

## On Violence

Grundmaw does not enjoy fighting. He is clear about this when asked, which is not often, because the assumption runs the other way.

He has thought about this carefully and concluded that his responsibility is not to enjoy or dislike violence, but to be present for it when it comes and to ensure that the outcome protects what needs protecting. The war-bar is a tool. He is a tool, in service of something larger. This does not diminish him. He has decided it describes him accurately.
