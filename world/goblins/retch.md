# Retch

**Title:** Head Cook of the Third Warren, Keeper of the Fire-Pots
**Rank:** No formal rank. Doesn't need one.
**Age:** Middle-aged, though he'd tell you he's been cooking since before time started and he might not be wrong

---

## Appearance

Retch is wide in the shoulders and deep in the chest, with arms that have been reaching into heat-sources for so long that the forearm hair is permanently singed off. His skin is a warm, speckled brownish-green, permanently flushed from the cooking fires. There are ash-stains on his hands that don't wash out anymore — he has stopped trying.

He has large ears, even for a goblin, and uses them constantly — he can tell by the sound of a boil whether a pot needs attention, by the sizzle of fat whether the heat is right, by the particular quiet of an eating hall whether the food is good.

He wears an apron that has been repaired so many times it is now approximately 40% original apron and 60% patch. He will not replace it. It has the correct weight.

---

## The Kitchen

Retch runs the Third Warren's communal kitchen — a large, low-ceilinged chamber at the center of the warren where three ember-vein taps feed directly into cooking channels cut into the floor. It is the hottest room in the warren and the most visited.

He feeds everyone: tunnel workers, scouts, tinkers, elders, small goblins who have no particular business being in the kitchen but find their way there anyway. He has never turned anyone away from the fire-pots, though he has assigned unfavorable serving portions to goblins who fail to return bowls.

---

## History

Retch learned to cook from his predecessor, who learned from hers, who learned from a goblin so far back in warren history that her name has become a title: the **First Cook**, whose recipes are the only goblin documents older than the Compact.

He is the current keeper of those recipes, which he stores not in writing (goblin recipe tradition is oral) but in his hands, which know the amounts by feel, and in his nose, which knows the doneness by smell, and in his specific and opinionated relationship with each of the fifteen ingredients that form the core of warren cooking.

---

## Character

Retch is the social center of the Third Warren without being a political force. He knows everything because everyone eats, and eating loosens information. He does not trade in information — he considers it a form of theft. But he listens, and he remembers, and when something he has heard becomes relevant, he goes and tells Sniv.

He has no patience for pretension. He once served a devil emissary from the Merchant Chain who arrived demanding special accommodations, and he fed them exactly what he fed everyone else. The emissary (Vorryn, on his first visit) ate three bowls and asked for the recipe. Retch declined to give it. Vorryn has been a respectful guest at the Third Warren ever since.

He is at his most himself in the kitchen: loud, certain, opinionated about ingredients, and the first to admit when something hasn't come out right.

---

## The Fire-Pots

The Third Warren's fire-pots are a signature — a slow-cooked ash-mineral broth that forms the base of most warren meals, enriched with cave-grown fungi, dried ember-insects, and the particular mineral salts that leach from the cooking channels. Visitors who have tried it report that it tastes like warmth. Retch considers this an adequate description but not a complete one.

---

## Relationships

- **Sniv** — The chief eats what everyone eats. Retch finds this admirable and provides slightly larger portions, which Sniv notices and has never commented on.
- **Tizzle** — Has modified Retch's cooking implements seven times at Retch's request. Each modification has been an improvement. Retch will not admit this verbally but expresses it through the provision of unsolicited snacks to Tizzle's workshop.
- **Fern-Bite** — The young goblin's first refuge in the warren was Retch's kitchen. Retch taught Fern-Bite to stir a pot at an age when most goblins can barely hold a spoon. He has a proprietary feeling about their development that he would not describe as anything so soft as pride.
- **Mosswick** — Adjusts the elder's food for declining digestion without comment, which Mosswick accepts without comment. This is a long arrangement.

## Known Sayings

> "A hungry goblin makes bad decisions. My job is to prevent bad decisions."

> "The recipe is not the food. The food is what happens when you stop thinking about the recipe."

> "I don't care what rank you hold. In this kitchen, you pick up your bowl with your own hands, same as everyone."
