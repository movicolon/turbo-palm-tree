# Mosswick

**Title:** Elder of the Third Warren, Keeper of the Oral Record
**Rank:** No rank. Beyond rank, technically. The rank system was devised after Mosswick was already old.
**Age:** The oldest goblin currently alive. She has stopped specifying.

---

## Appearance

Mosswick is small even for a goblin — a century of compression. She moves slowly and deliberately, in the way of something that has decided to still be here next year and is pacing accordingly.

Her skin has gone the dark, mottled green-grey of old lichen on ash-stone, with the particular texture of things that have weathered significantly and are not done weathering. Her eyes — pale gold, faded slightly at the edges — are the most alert things about her. They have been watching the Soot Kingdoms for a very long time.

Her hair is white, cut short. She wears simple clothes in dark colors and one piece of decoration: a cord around her wrist with seven knots, each tied at an event she will not name. The seventh was tied recently.

She moves with a carved wooden cane, which she uses when she chooses to and ignores when she doesn't. She is not always slow.

---

## The Oral Record

Every goblin warren has an oral historian. The Third Warren's is Mosswick, and has been for as long as the Third Warren has existed.

The Oral Record is everything the Registers are not: not the official history of the Soot Kingdoms, but the *actual* history — the events as they were experienced by goblins, the things that devil documents record poorly or not at all, the texture of life in the warrens across seven generations of change.

Mosswick knows the names and stories of every Warren-Chief since the Compact. She knows the names of the goblins who built the warrens, which tunnels are oldest, which ember-taps were dug by whose hands. She knows three versions of every significant event and the weight of evidence behind each.

She speaks Clatter with the fluency of someone who was speaking it before most of its current forms had evolved.

---

## History

Mosswick did not plan to be an elder. She planned to be a tunnel engineer, which she was for the first four decades of her life. She built sections of the Third Warren that are still standing, which she considers her primary achievement and which she never mentions.

She became an elder gradually, the way the most important things happen — through outlasting, through accumulating, through being the person who remembered what others had forgotten. By the time anyone was calling her the Keeper of the Oral Record, she already was one.

She has seen four Warren-Chiefs rise and, in three cases, fall. She has seen the Compact violated and renewed more times than she cares to count. She has watched the ember-veins shift, the warrens expand, and the Ember Wastes change in ways that she is the only one old enough to have noticed.

---

## Character

Mosswick is patient with a patience that is not passive but disciplined — the patience of someone who has found that waiting is often the most precise action available.

She does not give advice unless asked. She does not withhold it when asked. She is honest in a way that is not always comfortable, which is a quality that young goblins find difficult and come to be grateful for.

She is not sentimental about the past, which surprises people who expect it of her. She is interested in the past because it explains the present and illuminates the future. Sentiment, she says, is what you feel when you're not paying attention.

---

## The Seventh Knot

The seventh knot was tied the day Tizzle told Mosswick about the vein-voice. She has not explained this to anyone. When asked about the cord, she says only that the knots are for things worth remembering.

---

## Relationships

- **Sniv** — Her chief. She has served three chiefs, and Sniv is the best of them. She has not told Sniv this. She tells Sniv things that will make her better at her job.
- **Lucaveth** — Forty years of correspondence. She considers him the most intellectually honest devil she has met, which is a carefully worded compliment.
- **Ashgravel** — They share something that most beings in the Soot Kingdoms don't: they have both been listening to the ash for long enough to hear something. They meet irregularly. The conversations are short and significant.
- **Fern-Bite** — She sees in the young goblin something she cannot fully articulate and has decided to watch.
- **Retch** — He adjusts her food without comment. She accepts without comment. This is the most functional relationship in the warren.
