# Fern-Bite

**Title:** None yet. Working on it.
**Rank:** Junior Scout, Third Warren
**Age:** Young. The exact age is complicated because Fern-Bite was found, not born into, the Third Warren.

---

## Appearance

Fern-Bite is still growing, which among young goblins is an ongoing process with unpredictable timing. Currently: medium-small, lighter in coloring than most Third Warren goblins — a pale moss-green, almost grey in low light — which suggests origin from one of the outer warrens or possibly the Ember Wastes themselves.

Their eyes are the unusual pale color of ember-light through thin ash-stone: not gold like most goblins but almost silver-white. Other goblins find this slightly uncanny. Fern-Bite has decided to find it interesting rather than troubling.

They are wiry in the way of things that move constantly, with the specific kind of coordination that suggests a childhood spent in irregular spaces — no flat floors, no consistent gravity, a lot of improvising.

Their name is self-given. The original name, if there was one, has not been shared.

---

## History

Fern-Bite arrived in the Third Warren eight years ago, alone, from the direction of the Ember Wastes. They were small and had clearly been managing on their own for some time, which in the outer Wastes is notable survivorship for a young goblin.

They were brought to Retch, who fed them, and to Sniv, who asked three questions and then assigned them warren residency on a probationary basis. The probationary period was technically six months. It has not been formally closed, which Fern-Bite takes as either still pending or effectively forgotten.

They don't talk about before. The warren has, largely, decided not to ask.

---

## Character

Fern-Bite is intensely observant and deeply uncertain, which is an uncomfortable combination to inhabit but a useful one to be. They notice things — details, patterns, inconsistencies — and they sit with them longer than most goblins would before deciding what they mean.

They are attached to the Third Warren in the way of someone who was not sure they deserved a place and has been slowly, empirically, determining that they do. They are not fully confident in this conclusion. They are working on it.

They are drawn to edges — the edge of the warren, the edge of the Wastes, the edge of what is known. Not recklessly; they are careful. But the boundary is where they orient.

---

## The Outer Circles Incidents

Fern-Bite has been caught twice in Tormenex's Outer Circle facilities — not trying to break anyone out, not stealing, but clearly just *exploring*. Tormenex released them both times with a written warning.

They have thought about this a great deal. They are not sure what to make of Tormenex. They are not sure what Tormenex makes of them. They have not gone back. They have not stopped thinking about it.

---

## The Ashgravel Connection

Ashgravel is the only person outside the warren who knows Fern-Bite's history. Or part of it. This is not a small thing.

Fern-Bite sometimes follows Ashgravel on Wastes forays, keeping enough distance that Ashgravel could pretend not to notice. Ashgravel does not pretend. He does not ask questions, which is the only thing that makes the following feel safe.

---

## Relationships

- **Retch** — The first person who fed them. This is a significant relationship for reasons that don't reduce to words well.
- **Mosswick** — The elder watches Fern-Bite with an expression that Fern-Bite cannot read. They find this simultaneously unsettling and, in some way they can't explain, encouraging.
- **Ashgravel** — A connection that doesn't have a name for itself yet.
- **Pip-Scrabble** — Fern-Bite admires the scout enormously and has decided not to imitate her, because imitation would mean performing a version of exploration they haven't earned yet.
- **Tormenex** — Complicated. Unexplained grace from someone with no structural reason to give it. Fern-Bite does not know what to do with this and has decided to carry it carefully.

---

## What's Next

Fern-Bite is becoming something. The shape isn't clear yet, even to them.

Mosswick has tied a knot in her cord for them. She has not told them.

---

## Known Sayings

> "I came from somewhere. I'm still working out if that matters."

> "The edge isn't dangerous because things fall off it. It's dangerous because you can see from there, and once you've seen, you can't un-see."

> "Tormenex let me go. I've been thinking about that for two years. I don't know what it means. I think it means something."
