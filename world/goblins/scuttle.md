# Scuttle

**Title:** Chief Messenger of the Third Warren, Runner of the Bound Tongue Post
**Rank:** Messenger-First (the only Messenger-First; he designed the rank to ensure no one could be above him in his specialty)
**Age:** Indeterminate. He won't say. There are theories.

---

## Appearance

Scuttle is built for hallways. He is narrow, quick, and has the slightly diagonal posture of someone perpetually mid-transit. His limbs are disproportionately long — longer arms than goblins usually have, longer legs — which gives him a strange, loping gait that is faster than it looks and difficult to keep up with.

His skin is a dusty, warm tan-green, lighter than Third Warren goblins typically are, which suggests either mixed origin or significant time spent in environments without heavy ash-concentration. Both theories are probably partially correct.

He carries a satchel — always the same satchel, dark leather, heavily worn at the strap, with an intricate clasp mechanism that is the only piece of goblin-craft he has made himself. No one has seen inside it except the people it was opened for.

His most notable feature: he is always slightly out of breath, even when standing still, as though he just arrived and is about to leave.

---

## History

Scuttle has been running messages since before the Third Warren remembers. His origin is unclear — he arrived, as many important things in the warren have, without clear precedent.

He formalized the messenger system about forty years ago, transforming it from an ad hoc arrangement (send a quick goblin with a note) into a structured service with protocols, timings, and a code system he developed for sensitive communications. The code is based on Clatter but modified with additional tonal registers that he has not shared with anyone. He is the code.

He runs messages between all parts of the warren, to other warrens, and — most significantly — to devil territory. He is one of the few goblins with reliable, uncontested access to First Circle buildings. No one has formally granted this access. It has simply never been revoked, and removing it would disrupt information flow that multiple parties depend on.

---

## Character

Scuttle knows things. This is his primary characteristic. He carries messages and in doing so becomes a nexus of information, and he is not neutral about this — he manages it, actively, in ways that are not betrayals but are something more complex than simple service.

He has decided, at some point in his career, that the most important thing a messenger can do is ensure that the *right* messages arrive — not all messages, not the messages that would destabilize the fragile arrangements he has spent decades maintaining, but the right ones, in the right order, to the right recipients.

He has never opened a sealed message. He has on two occasions ensured that a message arrived one day later than it could have. On three occasions he has delivered a message and then immediately gone to the recipient's counterparty and said, without revealing the message's content, "there is a conversation you should be part of." He considers this within the parameters of his role. Others might not.

He is pleasant, efficient, and impossible to slow down.

## The Register Room

Scuttle is the only person other than Dusk-Elowen who regularly enters the Register Room. He brings her the day's devil-side correspondence. She gives him sealed responses. They have never had a conversation, by his report, longer than the time it takes to exchange items.

Dusk-Elowen's report would not differ on this point, which is interesting.

## Relationships

- **Sniv** — Operates within the chief's authority and reports genuinely on matters affecting warren security. Sniv trusts him within a careful boundary that both understand.
- **Dusk-Elowen** — Access. Regularity. A form of mutual recognition across a significant species divide. Something is there, but both parties have elected not to name it.
- **Seraphex** — Scuttle is not Seraphex's informant, which Seraphex knows. Seraphex is, however, occasionally useful to Scuttle's message-management, which Seraphex does not know. The information flows in one direction and it is not the one Seraphex assumes.
- **Pip-Scrabble** — Two different intelligence systems operating in the same space. They have a professional non-aggression arrangement: neither maps the other's network, both alert the other to genuine threats. It is the most functional intelligence relationship in the Soot Kingdoms.

---

## The Code

Scuttle's modified Clatter code is the most secure communication system in the Soot Kingdoms. He has had offers to formalize it, teach it, and weaponize it. He has declined all three.

When asked why, he says the code only works because only one person knows it. The moment it becomes a system, it becomes a vulnerability.

He has been asked whether he has a successor. He says not yet.
