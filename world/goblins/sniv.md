# Sniv

**Title:** Chief of the Scrabble-Warrens, First Headbutt of the Compact
**Rank:** Warren-Chief, Third Warren (the largest)
**Age:** Old for a goblin. Older than anyone expected.

---

## Appearance

Sniv is the size of a large dog and the presence of a small mountain. She has held the chieftaincy of the Third Warren for longer than most goblins have been alive, and it shows: her posture is authority condensed into a body built low and close to the ground.

Her skin is the dark greenish-grey of deep ash-rock, roughened at the elbows and knuckles from decades of tunnel work. One tusk is slightly chipped — she chipped it personally, against the Smudge Stone, during the Compact renewal forty years ago, and has never had it filed. It is, she says, a reminder.

Her eyes are the amber-gold of ember-light, and they miss nothing. She can hold eye contact with devils, which most goblins find physiologically difficult and all of them find politically inadvisable.

---

## History

Sniv became chief of the Third Warren by outlasting three predecessors, outworking six rivals, and building, piece by piece, the largest goblin political coalition the warrens have seen.

She has been held in Tormenex's Outer Circle facilities twice — once for a Compact violation (a shipment of devil-iron that she maintains she thought was legitimately salvaged) and once for a dispute that she refuses to characterize. Both times she was released exactly on schedule. She considers Tormenex the most reliable devil she has ever met, which says something about both of them.

She negotiated the current Ash Council structure — the three-three arrangement with the non-voting ember tap — in a session that lasted eleven days. The devils thought they were wearing her down. She was waiting for their energy to exhaust itself, which it did, on day nine.

---

## Character

Sniv leads by knowing more and waiting longer than anyone else in the room.

She speaks Clatter fluently, as expected, but she also speaks a rough, accented Bound Tongue — enough to follow devil negotiations without a translator. She has never revealed this to any devil except Malachar, and only then because Malachar already knew and she could see no strategic advantage in pretending otherwise.

She is practical about the Compact. It is not a friendship treaty. It is a tool, and tools require maintenance. She maintains it.

She is deeply loyal to the warrens and to goblins generally, but she distinguishes carefully between what goblins *want* and what they *need*, and she has made decisions that served the second at the expense of the first enough times that some goblins resent her. She accepts this as the cost of leadership.

---

## Relationships

- **Malachar** — Mutual, wary respect. The only relationship in the Soot Kingdoms where both parties know roughly what the other party knows. This makes their interactions unusually direct.
- **Mosswick** — Her oldest advisor. She learned most of what she knows about devil history from Mosswick's oral records. She defers to Mosswick on matters of precedent, which the elder finds touching and correct.
- **Grundmaw** — Her strongest warrior and occasionally her biggest political problem. She values Grundmaw absolutely on the field and keeps them at arm's length in council.
- **Tormenex** — Respect without warmth. He ran a fair sentence. She was impressed and has not forgotten.

---

## The Headbutt

When goblins renew the Compact, the ratification is performed with a headbutt to the Smudge Stone. The First Headbutt is done by the Warren-Chief. It is ceremonial — though, as Sniv notes, it is harder to be dishonest in a ceremony that leaves a mark on your forehead.

She has done the First Headbutt four times. Her forehead has a slight callus. She is, privately, proud of it.
