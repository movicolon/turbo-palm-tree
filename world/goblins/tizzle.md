# Tizzle

**Title:** Master Tinker of the Third Warren, Clan-Head of the Ash-Rivet Tinkers
**Rank:** Tinker-Master (the Tinker Clans rank independently from warren hierarchy)
**Age:** Hard to say. Tinkers stop aging the way other goblins do when they get deep enough into a project.

---

## Appearance

Tizzle is the kind of goblin who always has at least three things attached to her that weren't there yesterday. Currently: a pair of lenses mounted on a copper headband, two small tools tucked behind each ear, a bracer on the right forearm with a retractable probe, and a smear of something orange-yellow across her left cheek that has been there for four days and which she has not noticed.

Her hands are her most expressive feature: small, precise, constantly in motion — tapping, measuring invisible distances, adjusting the angle of things. She has burn marks on all her fingers and she can't feel hot and cold reliably anymore, which she considers an acceptable trade.

Her workshop coat is reinforced at the elbows, weighted at the hem (tools in the pockets), and has seventeen pockets. She knows what's in each one.

---

## History

Tizzle began her tinker career by taking apart everything in her family's quarters and reassembling it, slightly improved, while her parents slept. This was, among the Tinker Clans, considered a strong sign.

She was apprenticed at seven, Clan-Head at twenty-eight — the youngest in the Ash-Rivet Clan's history. She built her reputation on a series of modifications to the Third Warren's ember-vein tap system that increased heat output by thirty percent without increasing fuel consumption, a result that the First Circle asked her to explain and which she declined to do on the grounds that the explanation would take longer than it was worth.

She has a theory she is working on — something about the resonance of ember-vein channels and the potential for directed output — that Lucaveth has been trying to get her to write down for six years. She has explained, six times, that writing it down would solidify it prematurely. She is still working on it. She knows when she'll know it's done.

---

## Character

Tizzle is one of the most intelligent beings in the Soot Kingdoms, and she has the intellectual manners of someone who has never needed to perform intelligence for an audience. She is not impressive in conversation. She is devastating in application.

She has strong opinions about tools (goblin salvage is more creative; devil-forged is more precise; the ideal tool combines both and she is working on that) and weak opinions about politics, which she considers a poorly designed system that she has not been asked to improve and is not sure she could.

She is genuinely delighted by problems. Stuck mechanisms, inefficient systems, unexplained phenomena — she approaches them all with the same expression: the slight forward lean and the unfocused gaze of someone whose attention has gone somewhere more interesting than the room.

---

## The Theory

Tizzle's ongoing work concerns what she calls "the vein-voice" — a pattern of vibration she has detected in the ember-veins that she believes is not thermal noise but signal. She cannot yet decode it. She is building a tool to help.

When Lucaveth explained his biological hypothesis to her, she was quiet for a long time and then asked: "Does it have a period?" He did not know what she meant. She did not explain. She went back to her workshop and was not seen for three days.

## Relationships

- **Lucaveth** — Mutual regard across species, which both find slightly awkward and both have decided to get over. He provides historical data; she provides engineering insight. The collaboration has produced two breakthrough analyses.
- **Vorryn** — He declared her a peer publicly. She found this useful for supply chain access and mildly irritating for the attention it brought.
- **Retch** — The kitchen's equipment is impeccably maintained. She and Retch have an exchange economy with no currency: she fixes things, he feeds her. It is the most equitable relationship in the warren.
- **Sniv** — Reports to the chief on engineering matters. Sniv leaves her largely alone because the work is good and interference would slow it down. Tizzle has never said thank you for this, but she has quietly made the chief's quarters exceptionally energy-efficient.
